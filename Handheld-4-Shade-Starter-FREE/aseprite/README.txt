ASEPRITE SOURCE FILES
=====================
One .aseprite file for each animated sprite in this pack (8 files), laid out in the
same folders as the PNGs.

- Every animation is a named tag (idle, walk, attack ...) with its frame timing set,
  so you can press play straight away or export single animations.
- Sprites with a dark outline are split into two layers, "Outline" and "Colour",
  so you can recolour the body or restyle the outline on its own.
- The colour palette is loaded and the grid is set to the frame size.

Open them in Aseprite 1.3 or later (LibreSprite also works).
Stacked together the layers match the original PNG frames pixel for pixel.
The PNG files in the pack are unchanged.
