Echoes Of Mossbell - Individual Character Portraits
====================================================
Each character is saved as an individual PNG, cropped directly from your original image.
Files character_01.png through character_08.png are 144x144 original crops.
Files character_01_4x.png through character_08_4x.png are 576x576 nearest-neighbor enlargements.
Order: left-to-right across the top row, then left-to-right across the bottom row.
Use the original 144x144 files in your game code, or the 4x versions if your UI needs larger portraits.
